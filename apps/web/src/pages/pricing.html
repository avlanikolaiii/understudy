Questions · answers describe the design

Before you ask

Does any of this work yet?

Not yet. Understudy is a product concept, and everything on this page shows the design. We're building a first prototype of one workflow: it reads a Google Sheet, writes a report from a fixed template, rehearses on weeks it hasn't seen, and produces a receipt. It gets listed as working only once we can show it working. See Where Understudy stands.

Can a rehearsal change anything in my accounts?

No. During a rehearsal, connectors run in read mode. Understudy writes its version inside the app only. It doesn't save files, update trackers, or create drafts in your accounts.

How do you stop it from copying the answer during rehearsal?

It's only given the inputs that existed that week: the campaign data, the notes, and your template. The report you sent and your email are held back until its version is finished, and they're used only to compare.

What's the difference between “Done” and “Verified”?

“Done” is the status: what Understudy says it did. “Verified” is evidence: Understudy went back, read the result from the app, and it matched. A step can be done but not verifiable, and the receipt says so instead of hiding it.

What if an app changes its layout?

Steps that run through a connector don't depend on the screen, so a redesign shouldn't affect them. If a connector itself stops working, the run is designed to stop, mark the step Blocked, and say why in the receipt.

Why agencies first?

It's our starting hypothesis, not a limit. Agencies repeat the same client work every week across several apps, and most use Macs. If your team has a similar weekly routine, join early access and tell us about it.

Can it see my passwords?

No. macOS marks password fields as secure, and Understudy never records what you type into them. Connectors sign in through each app's own login.
