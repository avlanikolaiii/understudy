Rehearsal Concept demonstration

It proves it learned before you hand anything over

Copying what you just did proves nothing. So Understudy goes back to earlier weeks, gets only the inputs you had at the time, and writes its own version. Then you see the difference.

  • Blind. It gets that week's campaign data and notes, never the report you actually sent. Your finished work is only used afterward, to compare.
  • Read-only. Connectors run in read mode during rehearsal. Nothing is sent, saved, or updated in your live files, tracker, or inbox.
  • Side by side. You see every match and every difference before you decide to hand it off, rehearse again, or correct it.

Why not just ask ChatGPT or Claude?

Other tools follow instructions. Understudy learns your way and proves it.
