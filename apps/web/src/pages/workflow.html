How it works

Show it. Watch it rehearse. Hand it off.

No prompts or flowcharts. You teach it the way you'd teach a new colleague, and you check its work before you trust it.

1

Show it

Start Understudy, then do the update your usual way. It notes each app, field, and value you use. It doesn't record video of your screen.

It rehearses

It redoes past weeks from the raw inputs alone, without seeing what you sent, and without touching anything live. You compare its version with yours, side by side.

Hand it off

From then on it runs when you ask, or every Monday on its own. Every run ends with a receipt: what it did, and the evidence it checked.

Memory Concept demonstration

It remembers each client, so it doesn't guess

Every task and correction teaches Understudy something: who approves what, how each client likes their update, which words you never use. When you correct it, you choose how far the correction reaches, so one client's rule never leaks into another's report.

  • Scoped corrections. Just this once, for this client, or everywhere. Planned
  • Sources shown. Every memory says where it came from, and you can fix a wrong one in a click.
  • Stored on your Mac as plain files you can read, edit, or wipe.

Connectors Concept demonstration

Learns by watching. Runs through connectors.

Clicking through apps is slow and breaks when a button moves. So Understudy links each step you showed it to that app's connector and runs it directly. It calls an AI model only where the step needs judgment, which keeps runs fast and cheap.
