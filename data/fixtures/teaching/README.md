# Teaching data

Synthetic. Faro Creative (a fictional agency) and its fictional client Norte Studio.

- `sheet/campaign_data.csv`: the client's campaign sheet as it looked on the Monday of week 3, covering weeks 0–3.
- `sheet/notes.csv`: the sheet's Notes tab. It's part of the same spreadsheet, so there is still only one source.
- `reports/week-01.md` … `week-03.md`: the updates the agency "sent". Week 1 is the one demonstrated to Understudy. Weeks 2–3 are available during development.

Everything here may be read by developers and by the learning code.
