What we collect, and why

This site has one job: collecting interest in Understudy before it launches. Here's everything it does with your information.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the list.
  • Optional answers, only if you give them: what describes you best, what Mac you use, and the weekly work you'd hand off first.
  • Which form you used (home page or early-access page) and when you joined.

No cookies, no analytics, and no ad trackers. The form remembers your signup only in this browser tab, so it can ask the optional questions.

Why

To tell you when the first build is ready, invite you to early access, and decide what to build first. We don't sell or share the list, and we won't send marketing for anything else.

Where it's stored

The list is stored with Supabase in its São Paulo, Brazil region. Visitors can only add themselves; nobody can read the list through this site. The site is hosted by Vercel, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Both can see standard request data such as your IP address.

How long, and your choices
