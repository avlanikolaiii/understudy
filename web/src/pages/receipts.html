Receipts Concept demonstration

What it did, and how it checked, kept apart

“Done” is Understudy's claim. Evidence is what it found when it went back and looked. Every receipt shows both separately, so you know what to trust and what to open yourself.

Status: what it did

  • DoneFinished the step.
  • Partly doneFinished part of it. The rest depends on something missing.
  • WaitingStopped where you asked to approve.
  • BlockedCouldn't or wouldn't continue. The receipt says why.

Evidence: how it checked

  • VerifiedRead the result back from the app through its connector.
  • Partly verifiedSome checks passed, others couldn't be read back.
  • Not verifiableNothing reliable to check against. Look at it yourself.

Privacy and control · design rules

Always there. Only watching when you ask.

An app that sits on your screen has to earn the space. These rules are part of the design. Understudy will be open source, so you'll be able to check them in the code.

It saves steps, not screen video

It notes which app, field, and value you used. It never records what you type into password fields.

It only watches when you start it

The dot in the notch pulses whenever it's watching. If the dot isn't pulsing, it isn't looking.

It asks before anything leaves

Sending, paying, and deleting always wait for your OK. Click the notch at any time to stop a run.

Local unless you choose the cloud Cloud: Planned

Skills, memory, and receipts live on your Mac. Cloud AI and scheduled runs while your Mac sleeps will be opt-in parts of Pro.
